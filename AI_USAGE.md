# AI Usage and Authorship

Henry Barrientos originated the goal of turning his stellar-signal work into a
tool that could help astronomers, provided the project direction and computing
resources, operated and reviewed the workflow, selected the priority outcomes,
and is responsible for explaining and representing the work.

OpenAI Codex made substantial contributions to software implementation,
testing, documentation, benchmark operations, repository curation, and product
framing. Henry should not claim that every line was written without AI
assistance.

The defensible record consists of Henry's project decisions, dated history,
frozen protocols, hashes, failures, completion audits, test results, explicit
AI disclosure, and ability to explain the method and its limitations.

Before a scientific submission or customer delivery, a qualified human should
review the code, claims, and results. AI output is not a scientific endorsement
or a substitute for domain review.

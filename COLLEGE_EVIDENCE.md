## 60-second explanation

I began with a reproducible investigation of eclipse-like events associated
with TIC 31065777 in public TESS data. I wanted to scale the approach, so I
froze a 1,000-target benchmark instead of selecting examples that made the
method look good. The benchmark completed 2,180 method records and passed
matching 22-check local and server audits, but the scientific result was mixed:
OMEGA exactly recovered only 9 of 300 positive test periods, and neither of the
two real-catalog positives. I preserved that negative result and changed the
question. Instead of claiming a universal detector, I developed an auditable
review workflow that keeps failures, hashes, ranked events, and a reviewer
table. My next test is whether it saves astronomers review time without hiding
events they consider important. The project taught me that research is not
making the biggest claim; it is building the strongest test that can prove you
wrong and changing direction when the evidence does.
